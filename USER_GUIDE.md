## 1. What is Dine At Night?

**Dine At Night** is Lagos' first nighttime food market — a curated outdoor event where food meets culture under neon lights. The website is your home for:

- Discovering upcoming events and buying tickets
- Finding and applying as a food vendor
- Browsing event photos and videos
- Shopping official Dine At Night merchandise
- Reading and sharing attendee experiences

## 3. Buying Event Tickets

### Step-by-Step

1. Go to **Events** from the navigation menu
2. Find the event you want to attend (active events appear at the top with a countdown timer)
3. Click **"Get Tickets"** on the event card
4. A ticket form will appear. Fill in:
   - **Full Name** — your real name (printed on the ticket)
   - **Email Address** — where your e-ticket will be sent
   - **Phone Number** — for order verification
   - **Ticket Type** — choose between available tiers (e.g. General, VIP, VVIP) and their prices
   - **Quantity** — how many tickets (up to 20 per order)
5. Click **"Proceed to Payment"**
6. You'll be taken to **Paystack's** secure payment page
7. Complete payment with your card or bank transfer
8. You'll be redirected back to the site with your **e-ticket**

### What Happens After Payment
- You'll land on your **e-ticket page** with a QR code
- A confirmation email is sent to the address you provided
- Bookmark or screenshot your e-ticket page — the URL is unique to your ticket
- **Your QR code is your entry pass** — show it at the gate

### Ticket Types
| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **General Admission** | Standard entry to the event |
| **VIP** | Priority access + perks (varies per event) |
| **VVIP** | Exclusive access + premium experience (limited spots) |

### Important Notes
- **Tickets are non-refundable** unless the event is cancelled by Dine At Night
- One QR code = one entry. The system marks it as "scanned" on first use
- If you buy multiple tickets in one order, you receive one QR code covering the full quantity
- Tickets are linked to the email and name you provided — bring valid ID to events

## 4. Viewing Your E-Ticket

Your e-ticket is at: `dineatnight.com/tickets/{your-reference-code}`

The reference code is in your confirmation email (subject: *"Your Ticket — [Event Name]"*).

Your e-ticket shows:
- Event name, date, venue
- Your name and email
- Number of tickets
- Amount paid
- Your **QR code** (used for gate entry)

### At the Gate
- Open your e-ticket page on your phone **before joining the queue**
- Make sure your screen brightness is high
- The staff member with a scanner will scan your QR code
- You'll see "Confirmed ✓" and you're in

### If You Can't Find Your E-Ticket
- Check your email inbox and spam/junk folder for the confirmation email
- The subject line is: **"Your Ticket — [Event Name] | Dine At Night"**
- Contact us via the **Contact** page with your order reference

## 5. Applying as a Vendor

### Who Can Apply
Any food, drink, or food-adjacent business is welcome to apply. We review all applications and prioritise:
- Food quality and variety
- Brand presentation
- Alignment with the Dine At Night vibe

### Application Process

1. Click **"Apply as Vendor"** — the button appears on the Home, Events, Vendors, and About pages
2. A form will open. Fill in two sections:

**Section 1 — Your Brand**
- Brand Name
- Owner/Contact Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Instagram handle (optional but recommended)
- **Food Category** — select up to 3 that describe your offerings
- Which event you're applying for

**Section 2 — More About Your Brand**
- Description of your brand and what makes you unique
- Main image URL (a clear photo of your food or booth)
- Logo URL (optional)
- Menu builder — add your menu categories and items with prices

3. Review your information in the **confirmation step** (step 3)
4. Submit the application

### After You Apply
- You'll receive an **acknowledgement email** immediately
- Our team reviews all applications within **3–5 business days**
- You'll receive a **decision email** (approved / not approved)
- If approved, our team will contact you with booth assignment, setup time, and logistics

### If You're Declined
- It is not necessarily permanent — we run multiple editions per year
- You are welcome to reapply for a future edition
- If a reason was provided in your email, use it to strengthen your next application

### Re-Applying
The system recognises returning applicants by email. Your previous application information is partially saved, making it faster to re-apply. Just update what has changed.

### What to Avoid
- Do not submit multiple applications for the same event with different emails
- Do not use stock photos — use real photos of your food
- Do not exaggerate your menu — it affects your booth assignment
- Placeholder image URLs (broken links) will slow down your review

## 6. Shopping the Merch Store

### Browsing Products

1. Go to **Shop** from the navigation
2. Use the category tabs to filter: **All / T-Shirts / Hoodies / Caps / Tote Bags / Stickers / Limited**
3. Hover over a product card to see the 3D tilt effect and more details
4. Look out for:
   - **🔥 Hot Pick** badge — the current best-seller
   - **Limited** badge — limited edition item, get it while it lasts
   - **Sold Out** pill — item is currently out of stock; hovering also shows "Out of Stock" in the overlay
5. If someone else buys the last unit while you're browsing, sold-out items are **automatically removed from your cart** in real-time

### Adding to Cart

1. Click **"Add to Cart"** on any product
2. The cart drawer opens on the right side
3. Adjust quantities with the `+` / `–` buttons
4. To remove an item, reduce quantity to 0 or click the remove button
5. Your cart total updates automatically

### Checkout

1. Click **"Checkout"** in the cart drawer
2. Fill in the checkout form:
   - **Full Name**
   - **Email Address** — where your order confirmation will be sent
   - **Phone Number**
   - **Delivery Address** — street, city, state (for physical delivery)
3. Click **"Pay with Paystack"**
4. Complete payment on the Paystack page
5. You'll be redirected to a confirmation page with your **order summary**

### After Purchase
- You're redirected to an **order confirmation page** with your full order summary
- The confirmation page shows a **live delivery tracker** — check back at any time to see if your order is Pending, Dispatched, or Delivered
- Our team will process and dispatch your order
- You can return to your confirmation page at any time using the link in your order confirmation email

### Tracking Your Delivery

Your confirmation page (`/shop/verify?reference=...`) shows a progress bar:
1. **Order Placed** — payment confirmed, order received
2. **Dispatched** — your package is on its way
3. **Delivered** — package received

If your order is marked **Returned**, this is shown separately with an orange indicator.

### Notes
- Your cart is **saved to your browser** (localStorage) — it persists through page refreshes and browser restarts on the same device
- If a product sells out while items are in your cart, those items are automatically removed
- Prices are in **Nigerian Naira (₦)**
- All prices shown are the final price (no additional Paystack fees to you)

## 7. Browsing the Gallery

1. Go to **Gallery** from the navigation
2. By default you see all media from all events
3. Use the **filter bar** at the top to filter by:
   - **Event** — see only photos/videos from a specific event
   - **Type** — Photos only or Videos only

### Viewing Photos
- Click any photo to open it in **fullscreen lightbox**
- Use the left/right arrows (or swipe on mobile) to navigate between photos
- Press Escape or click outside to close

### Watching Videos
- Videos play **inline** in the gallery grid — click the play button
- Videos loop automatically
- Click outside the video or scroll away to stop playback

## 8. Leaving a Review

We love hearing from attendees and vendors! You can leave a review on the **Home**, **Events**, **Vendors**, and **About Us** pages.

### How to Submit a Review

1. Scroll to the **"What People Say"** section on any of those pages
2. Fill in the form:
   - **I am a** — choose "Event Attendee" or "Vendor"
   - **Your Name / Brand Name** — your name (attendees) or brand name (vendors)
   - **Which event?** — optional, select the event you attended
   - **Your Review** — share your experience (10–400 characters)
3. Click **"Submit Review →"**
4. Your review goes live instantly in the carousel

### Review Guidelines
- Keep it honest and based on your real experience
- No hate speech, personal attacks, or offensive content
- All reviews are visible to the public immediately

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I paid for tickets but didn't receive a confirmation email.**
A: Check your spam/junk folder. The subject line is "Your Ticket — [Event Name] | Dine At Night". If it's not there, contact us with your payment reference number (visible on your bank statement or Paystack receipt).

**Q: Can I get a refund on my ticket?**
A: Tickets are non-refundable. If the event is cancelled by Dine At Night, you will be contacted with information about refunds or transfers.

**Q: I made a mistake in my name/email when buying a ticket. What do I do?**
A: Contact us immediately via the contact form or hello@dineatnight.com with your payment reference. We'll correct it before the event.

**Q: Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?**
A: Contact us with your reference and the name to transfer to. We will handle it manually.

**Q: I applied as a vendor — when will I hear back?**
A: We review all applications within 3–5 business days. Check the email you used to apply (including spam).

**Q: The shop checkout page shows an error after payment.**
A: Your payment may still have gone through. Check your bank or Paystack for a deduction, then contact us with the reference number shown on your Paystack receipt.

**Q: I submitted a contact form but didn't get a confirmation email.**
A: The confirmation email comes from the EmailJS system. Check spam. If it's not there, your message was likely still received — we'll respond regardless.

**Q: The website is slow or not loading.**
A: Try refreshing the page. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache (`Ctrl+Shift+R` or `Cmd+Shift+R`) or try a different browser.

**Q: The gallery videos won't play.**
A: Make sure you're not on a very slow connection. Videos are streamed from external URLs. Try refreshing or switching to a stronger network.

## 12. Troubleshooting

### Payment Didn't Go Through
1. Check if money left your account (via banking app or SMS)
2. If deducted but no ticket — contact us with the Paystack reference number
3. If not deducted — try again with a different card or bank transfer

### E-Ticket Page Shows "Not Found"
- Check the URL — ensure you copied it correctly from your email
- The URL format is: `dineatnight.com/tickets/[your-reference]`
- Try opening it in a fresh browser tab

### QR Code Not Scanning at Gate
- Increase your screen brightness to maximum
- Try expanding the page zoom in your browser
- Move to better lighting
- If all else fail, show the gate staff your confirmation email — they can look up your reference manually

### Vendor Form Won't Submit
- All required fields (marked with `*`) must be filled
- You must select at least one food category
- Image URLs must be real, working links (test them in a new tab first)
- Check your internet connection

### Contact Form Shows "Try Again"
- Wait 60 seconds (the form has a cooldown to prevent spam)
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different browser
- If persistent, email us directly at thecodehive.tch@gmail.com
